Samsung Galaxy M17e Review: Design, Display, Battery & Camera

By Dhuda Sathish • Updated March 16, 2026
Highlights
  • Samsung Galaxy M17e priced at ₹14,999 with 128GB storage
  • Features 6000mAh battery and 45W fast charging
  • 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display with Gorilla Glass 5
  • Powered by MediaTek Dimensity 7300 processor

Samsung has launched its latest budget-friendly smartphone, the Galaxy M17e, in the Indian market. This device aims to deliver premium features at an affordable price point, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious consumers.

The Galaxy M17e is priced at ₹14,999 for the base 128GB variant, while the 256GB model costs ₹16,999. This pricing strategy puts it in direct competition with devices from brands like Redmi, Realme, and Poco, but Samsung's brand value and feature set make it a compelling choice.

In this comprehensive review, we'll take a closer look at the Galaxy M17e's design, display performance, battery life, camera capabilities, and overall value proposition. We'll also compare it with its competitors to help you make an informed buying decision.

Unboxing

The Galaxy M17e comes in Samsung's signature eco-friendly packaging. The box is compact and minimalistic, featuring the device name and a small image of the phone. Samsung has been moving towards sustainable packaging, and the M17e follows this trend.

Inside the box, you'll find the Galaxy M17e smartphone, a 45W fast charger (a pleasant surprise at this price point), a USB Type-C cable, SIM ejector tool, and some basic documentation. Samsung including a fast charger in the box is a big plus, as many competitors have stopped including chargers with their devices.

The device itself feels premium right out of the box, with a glossy finish on the back and solid build quality. Samsung has paid attention to the unboxing experience, making it feel special even for a budget device.

Specifications

Here are the detailed specifications of the Samsung Galaxy M17e. The device features a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300 processor. It comes with up to 8GB RAM and 256GB storage.

The camera setup includes a 50MP main sensor with an 8MP ultra-wide lens and a 2MP macro camera. For selfies, there's a 13MP front camera. The device is backed by a massive 6000mAh battery with 45W fast charging support and runs on Android 15 with One UI 7.0 on top.

Design

The Galaxy M17e features a modern design language that doesn't scream "budget." The back panel has a glossy finish with a subtle gradient pattern that changes color based on the viewing angle. Samsung is offering the device in three colors: Black, Blue, and Silver.

The device measures 165.4 x 76.3 x 8.4mm and weighs 191 grams, making it comfortable to hold despite the large battery. The side-mounted fingerprint sensor is integrated into the power button and is both fast and accurate. The USB Type-C port and speaker grille are located at the bottom, while the 3.5mm headphone jack (a rare feature in 2026) sits at the top.

The frame is made of plastic but has a metallic finish that gives it a premium look. The camera module protrudes slightly but not enough to cause wobble on flat surfaces. Overall, the design feels well-thought-out and doesn't compromise on aesthetics despite the budget price tag.

Display

One of the standout features of the Galaxy M17e is its 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate. This is impressive at this price point, as most competitors offer LCD panels or 60Hz refresh rates in this segment.

The display produces vibrant colors with deep blacks, thanks to the AMOLED technology. The 120Hz refresh rate makes scrolling and animations incredibly smooth. The panel also supports HDR10+, enhancing the viewing experience for compatible content on streaming platforms like Netflix and YouTube.

With a peak brightness of 1000 nits, the display remains visible even under direct sunlight. The Gorilla Glass 5 protection adds durability against scratches and minor drops. The bezels are minimal, giving the device a modern look with a screen-to-body ratio of approximately 89%.

Performance

Under the hood, the Galaxy M17e is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7300 processor, built on a 6nm process. This chipset offers excellent performance for its price segment, handling daily tasks with ease and even managing some light gaming.

In benchmark tests, the device scores around 520,000 points in AnTuTu, which is competitive for this price range. Real-world performance is smooth, with no noticeable lag during app switching or multitasking. The 120Hz display contributes to the fluid user experience.

Gaming performance is decent for casual games. Titles like BGMI and Call of Duty Mobile run at medium settings with stable frame rates. However, for more demanding games, you might need to lower the graphics settings to maintain smooth performance. The device does not overheat during normal usage, though extended gaming sessions can make it warm to the touch.

Camera

The Galaxy M17e features a triple-camera setup on the back, led by a 50MP main sensor with f/1.8 aperture. The main camera captures detailed images in good lighting conditions, with natural color reproduction and good dynamic range.

The 8MP ultra-wide lens is decent for capturing landscapes and group shots, though there's some distortion at the edges. The 2MP macro camera is more of a marketing feature, producing average results that are usable only in perfect lighting conditions.

The 13MP front camera takes good selfies in daylight, with natural skin tones. In low light, both front and rear cameras struggle, producing noisy images, which is expected at this price point. The camera app offers various modes including Night mode, Portrait mode, and Pro mode for more control over settings.

Video recording is capped at 4K at 30fps from the main camera, while the front camera is limited to 1080p at 30fps. The electronic image stabilization (EIS) helps reduce shakes in videos, but it's not as effective as optical stabilization.

Battery

The Galaxy M17e packs a massive 6000mAh battery, which is one of its biggest selling points. In our testing, the device easily lasted a full day of heavy usage, with around 30-40% battery remaining at bedtime.

For moderate users, the device can easily last two days on a single charge. Even with continuous video playback, the device managed around 18 hours of screen-on time. Gaming for an hour consumed only about 12-15% of the battery, which is impressive.

The 45W fast charging is another highlight. The included 45W charger can charge the device from 0 to 100% in approximately 75 minutes. A quick 15-minute charge can give you about 30% battery, which is enough for several hours of use. The device also supports 15W wireless charging, a rare feature at this price point.

Software

The Galaxy M17e runs on Android 15 with Samsung's One UI 7.0 on top. Samsung has promised 4 years of Android updates and 5 years of security updates, which is excellent for a budget device.

One UI 7.0 brings several new features and improvements over previous versions. The interface is clean and intuitive, with minimal bloatware. Samsung has included some useful features like Game Booster, Secure Folder, and Samsung Pay.

The software optimization is good, with smooth animations and transitions. The 120Hz display is well-utilized by the software, making the overall experience fluid. Samsung has also included some exclusive features like Quick Share, which allows seamless file sharing with other Samsung devices.

Galaxy M17e vs Competitors

When compared to its competitors like the Redmi Note 13 Pro and Realme 12 Pro, the Galaxy M17e holds its own quite well. The AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh rate is a significant advantage over most competitors that offer LCD panels or 60Hz refresh rates.

The 6000mAh battery is larger than what most competitors offer at this price point. The inclusion of a 45W fast charger in the box is another advantage, as many competitors either don't include a charger or provide a slower one.

However, the Dimensity 7300 processor is slightly less powerful than the Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 found in some competitors. The camera performance is comparable, with each device having its strengths and weaknesses. Samsung's brand value and software update policy give the M17e an edge in the long run.

Final Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy M17e is an impressive budget smartphone that offers excellent value for money. With its AMOLED display, massive battery, and decent performance, it stands out in a crowded market.

The device is particularly suitable for users who prioritize battery life and display quality over raw performance. The inclusion of a fast charger and wireless charging support at this price point is commendable.

While it may not be the most powerful device in its price segment, the overall package is well-balanced. Samsung's brand value and承诺 of long-term software updates make it a safe investment for those looking for a reliable budget smartphone.

If you're in the market for a budget smartphone under ₹15,000 and prioritize display quality and battery life, the Galaxy M17e should definitely be on your shortlist. It offers a premium experience that punches above its weight class.
