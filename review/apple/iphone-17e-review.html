iPhone 17e Review: Unboxing, First Impressions & Comparison

By Dhuda Sathish • Updated March 16, 2026
Highlights
  • iPhone 17e priced at ₹64,900 with 256GB storage
  • Features A19 Chip and MagSafe support
  • 60Hz OLED display, No Dynamic Island
  • Better battery life than regular iPhone 17

This is the new budget smartphone coming from Apple, called the iPhone 17e. They call it a budget smartphone, but when you look at the price, it’s not really budget for everyone.

This phone has already gone on sale, so if you want, you can buy it now. The price of this phone is ₹64,900. If we compare it with last year’s iPhone 16, the price has increased by about ₹5,000. Last year, the iPhone 16 launched at ₹59,900, but that was the 128GB model. This one comes with 256GB storage and 8GB RAM, so it also supports Apple Intelligence features.

If we compare the iPhone 16 256GB model, which launched at ₹69,900, this new iPhone 17e at ₹64,900 is actually ₹5,000 cheaper. In this video, we are going to unbox this phone and see how it is. At the end, I will also tell you whether you should buy iPhone 16 or iPhone 17e, because both are available around the same price and many people may be confused.

Unboxing

First, let’s check the unboxing. Here is the iPhone 17e box, and Apple has also provided a silicone case for the iPhone 17e. This case supports MagSafe, which is actually a plus point. The iPhone 16 did not have MagSafe support, but the iPhone 17e does. Because of this, you can use MagSafe accessories and wireless chargers easily.

Opening the box is very easy. Apple designs its packaging so that you don’t even need a knife to open it. Once we open the box, the first thing we see is the phone itself. Earlier Apple used plastic wrapping, but now they use paper packaging, which is better for the environment, although the unboxing feel is slightly different. This is the black color iPhone 17e.

Inside the box, we also get a SIM ejector pin, a Type-C to Type-C braided cable, and some documentation with a QR code. Instead of printed manuals, Apple now provides a QR code that you can scan to read the documents online. Now let’s check the silicone MagSafe case. The price of this case is ₹4,900. The build quality is good. When we place the phone inside the case, it looks pretty nice.

Specifications

You can see all the specifications on the screen. The display is a 6.1-inch OLED panel with a 60Hz refresh rate. The processor is the Apple A19 chip. On the back, there is a 48MP camera, and on the front, there is a 12MP selfie camera.

The phone runs on iOS 26. The battery is around 4000mAh, with 20W fast charging support and 15W wireless charging.

Changes Compared to iPhone 16

If we compare this with the iPhone 16, there are a few changes. Last time we had Ceramic Shield protection, but this time Apple has introduced Ceramic Shield 2, which offers slightly improved protection. The processor has changed from A18 to A19.

Another big change is the base storage, which has increased from 128GB to 256GB. Also, earlier the wireless charging was 7.5W Qi charging, but now it supports MagSafe with up to 15W wireless charging. So the major difference between iPhone 16 and iPhone 17e is the MagSafe support.

Design

When it comes to design, there are basically no major changes. According to leaks and rumors, we expected Dynamic Island, but that is not present here. Instead, Apple is still using the old notch design, just like the iPhone 16.

The phone still has a single rear camera. We expected Apple to add another camera in this generation, but that didn’t happen. The frame is still aluminum, and the front and back are made of glass. The build quality feels premium when you hold it.

The display is still 6.1-inch OLED with a 60Hz refresh rate. I personally thought Apple might bring 120Hz, since the iPhone 17 models already have it, but this phone still uses 60Hz. The display is good for general usage, but if you have already used a 120Hz display, the difference is very noticeable. Apple calls its 120Hz display ProMotion, and that experience is definitely smoother.

Performance

The phone is powered by the A19 chip, the same processor used in the iPhone 17. However, there is a small difference in the GPU. iPhone 17 has a 5-core GPU, while the iPhone 17e (budget model) has a 4-core GPU. So there is a small performance difference.

You can still play games like BGMI or Call of Duty easily, but since the display is 60Hz, you can only get around 60 FPS. The phone is also slightly smaller, with a 6.1-inch display, while the iPhone 17 comes with a 6.3-inch display.

Camera

In terms of cameras, there are no major changes compared to iPhone 16. You still get a 48MP rear camera and 12MP front camera. The front camera is not extremely impressive, especially in low light. In daylight with good lighting, it performs well.

The rear camera is quite good, and you can record 4K video at 60fps from both front and back cameras.

Battery

The battery life is actually a strong point of this phone. It comes with around 4000mAh battery, while the iPhone 17 has around 3692mAh. Because the display is only 60Hz, the battery life is slightly better. You can expect around 1 hour more screen-on time compared to iPhone 17.

Charging supports 20W fast charging, and it takes about 1.5 hours to fully charge.

Software and Connectivity

The phone runs on iOS 26, and Apple’s software experience is already well known for being smooth. It includes all necessary sensors like Gyroscope, Magnetometer, and NFC. Connectivity includes Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.3, and USB-C (USB 2 speeds). Face ID is also present and works reliably.

iPhone 17e vs iPhone 17 (Regular)

Now if we compare this phone with the iPhone 17, the iPhone 17 costs around ₹77,000 with offers. If you add ₹10,000–₹15,000 more, I would recommend going for the iPhone 17 instead. Why? Because the iPhone 17 gives you a 120Hz ProMotion display, Dual cameras (48MP main + 48MP ultrawide), and an 18MP front camera.

It also supports Center Stage, Macro photography, Spatial photos, Cinematic mode, Action mode, Dual capture, Ultra-wideband chip, and Bluetooth 6. So overall, the iPhone 17 offers many more features. The only advantage of this phone is slightly better battery life.

Final Recommendation

If you want to buy an iPhone right now with a ₹60,000–₹70,000 budget, I recommend waiting for sale events like the Dasara sale. During those sales, the iPhone 17 may become cheaper, making it the better option.

I would actually suggest skipping both iPhone 16 and this budget iPhone 17e, and instead saving a little more money and buying the regular iPhone 17.
