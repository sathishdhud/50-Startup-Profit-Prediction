Nothing Phone (4) Pro Review: A Bold Gamble That Paid Off?

By Dhuda Sathish • Updated March 20, 2026
Highlights
  • Metal Unibody: Premium build quality rarely seen in this segment.
  • 144Hz Display: Smooth 1.5K panel with thin bezels.
  • Periscope Camera: 50MP sensor with 3.5x optical zoom.
  • Battery Life: 5400mAh cell offering exceptional Screen-On Time.

There is always a fine line with boldness in tech—you either fail miserably or succeed unimaginably. This defines Nothing's journey perfectly. While last year had its struggles, the Nothing Phone (4) Pro seems to hit the right spot. Following the success of the "4A", this new "Pro" model aims to blend unique design with high-end performance.

If summed up in three words, this phone is about design, cameras, and experience. It aims to feel premium in an era where plastic builds are common. Let's dive into the details to see if this gamble pays off.

Nothing Phone (4) Pro design and display

The Phone (4) Pro is arguably the best-looking device Nothing has made. It balances bold aesthetics with minimalism perfectly. The metal body and frame give it a premium heft, while the transparent camera island keeps the signature "Nothing" vibe alive with visible screws and a functional red LED light.

It is the slimmest Nothing phone yet, and rounded edges make it comfortable to hold. Durability is strong thanks to the metal unibody and Gorilla Glass 7i on the front. The front sports a 6.83-inch 1.5K panel with a 144Hz refresh rate. It is bright, sharp, and handles HDR well on YouTube, though Netflix HDR is missing. Thebezels are thin, but the lack of Dolby Atmos for speakers is a slight downside.

Nothing Phone (4) Pro performance and software

Powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 and up to 12GB RAM, the phone glides through daily tasks. Multitasking is smooth, and bugs are virtually non-existent. Nothing OS remains one of the cleanest interfaces—minimal, bloat-free, and fluid.

Gaming is solid, with PUBG Mobile running at high frame rates. However, heavy titles like Genshin Impact show minor struggle during intense scenes. The Glyph interface is customizable for utilities, and the promise of 3 years of OS updates ensures longevity.

Nothing Phone (4) Pro cameras

The highlight is the 50MP periscope lens with 3.5x optical zoom—a rare feature in this price range. It captures impressive shots with good color and detail. The main camera is reliable for day and night shots, offering natural colors.

Portraits are excellent with good edge detection, though low-light performance can struggle slightly. Selfies are decent, but video recording is limited to 4K 30fps, lacking the 4K 60fps option that the chipset supports.

Nothing Phone (4) Pro battery life and verdict

Battery life is a surprise strength. The 5400mAh battery easily delivers over 8 hours of Screen-On Time. It supports 50W fast charging (charger sold separately), but lacks wireless charging due to the metal body.

Priced at roughly ₹39,000, the Phone (4) Pro justifies its cost through experience rather than just specs. It combines a striking design, clean software, and great battery life. It is a highly recommended buy for those prioritizing aesthetics and usability.

Comparison: Nothing Phone (4) Pro vs OnePlus Nord 4

The closest competitor to the Phone (4) Pro is the OnePlus Nord 4. Both phones feature metal unibodies and focus on performance. However, Nothing takes the lead in camera versatility with its dedicated periscope zoom lens, which the Nord 4 lacks.

While the Nord 4 might offer slightly faster charging speeds, the Phone (4) Pro wins on display quality with a higher 144Hz refresh rate and a cleaner software experience without bloatware. If cameras and UI smoothness are your priority, the Nothing Phone (4) Pro is the better choice.

Questions and Answers

Does the Nothing Phone (4) Pro support 4K 60fps video?
No, the phone is currently limited to 4K 30fps on the main camera, despite the chipset supporting higher resolutions.
Is there wireless charging on this model?
No, due to the metal back design, wireless charging is not supported.
How good is the battery life?
It is excellent. You can expect over 8 hours of Screen-On Time with normal usage, easily lasting a full day and a half.
Does it have a headphone jack?
No, the Phone (4) Pro relies on USB Type-C for audio and does not include a 3.5mm jack.
