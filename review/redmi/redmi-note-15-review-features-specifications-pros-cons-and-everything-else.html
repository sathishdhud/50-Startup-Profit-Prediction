Redmi Note 15 Review: Design, Display, Performance, Camera, Battery and Price

By Dhuda Sathish | Reviewed by Maaney Editorial Team | Updated March 14, 2026
In This Review
  • Design and AMOLED Display
  • Performance and Gaming Test
  • 108MP Camera Review
  • Battery Life and Charging Speed
  • Pros and Cons

The Redmi Note 15 is Xiaomi’s latest mid-range smartphone designed to offer strong performance, a premium display, and a powerful camera at an affordable price. The phone combines a modern design with an AMOLED display, a capable Snapdragon processor, and a large battery.

Design and AMOLED Display

The Redmi Note 15 features a slim and lightweight design that feels more premium than many mid-range phones like the Samsung Galaxy A35 and Realme Narzo 70 Pro. On the front, the phone offers a large 6.77-inch AMOLED display with a smooth 120Hz refresh rate that makes scrolling, gaming, and video playback look fluid. The display also supports high peak brightness, which helps improve outdoor visibility. Overall, the combination of modern design and vibrant screen gives the Redmi Note 15 a strong advantage in its price segment.

Feature Redmi Note 15
Display Size 6.7-inch AMOLED
Resolution Full HD+
Refresh Rate 120Hz
Peak Brightness Up to 2000 nits

The 120Hz refresh rate ensures smoother scrolling and better animations. This makes the device feel faster during everyday usage.

Performance and Gaming Test

The Redmi Note 15 processor is designed to deliver smooth performance for everyday usage such as browsing, social media, and multitasking. Combined with up to 8GB RAM, the phone handles multiple apps without noticeable lag. Compared with phones like the Samsung Galaxy A35 and Realme Narzo 70 Pro, the performance remains competitive in the mid-range segment.

Feature Redmi Note 15
Processor Snapdragon chipset
RAM 6GB / 8GB
Storage 128GB / 256GB
Operating System HyperOS based on Android

In gaming tests, the Snapdragon chipset delivers stable frame rates in popular games like BGMI and Call of Duty Mobile on medium to high settings. The phone stays responsive during long gaming sessions, making it a reliable option for users who want balanced performance without moving to a higher-priced flagship device.

108MP Camera Review

The 108MP main camera on the Redmi Note 15 focuses on delivering detailed photos in both daylight and indoor conditions. The sensor captures sharp images with balanced colors and improved dynamic range compared to many mid-range phones like the Realme Narzo 70 Pro and Samsung Galaxy A35. Xiaomi’s image processing also enhances clarity in portraits and landscape shots.

Camera Specification
Main Camera 108MP primary sensor
Ultra-Wide 8MP lens
Front Camera 16MP selfie camera
Video Recording 4K video support

In real usage, the Redmi Note 15 camera performs well for social media photos, portraits, and everyday photography. Low-light performance is improved with night mode, while the front camera captures clear selfies suitable for video calls and content creation.

Battery Life and Charging Speed

The 5000mAh battery in the Redmi Note 15 delivers reliable battery life for everyday usage including social media, video streaming, and gaming. In real-world tests, the phone can comfortably last a full day and often extends close to one and a half days with moderate usage. Compared with devices like the Samsung Galaxy A35 and Realme Narzo 70 Pro, the battery performance remains competitive in the mid-range segment.

Feature Redmi Note 15
Battery Capacity 5000mAh
Charging 45W fast charging
Typical Usage Up to 1.5 days

The 45W fast charging helps the device recharge quickly, reducing waiting time when the battery runs low. This combination of strong battery capacity and fast charging makes the Redmi Note 15 suitable for users who rely on their phone throughout the day.

Pros, Cons and Final Verdict

The Redmi Note 15 offers a balanced experience with a bright display, reliable performance, and solid battery life. Xiaomi focuses on delivering a strong mid-range package that competes well with phones like the Samsung Galaxy A35 and Realme Narzo series. For users who want a modern smartphone for everyday usage, gaming, and photography, the device provides good overall value in its price segment.

Pros
  • AMOLED display with smooth 120Hz refresh rate
  • High-resolution 108MP main camera
  • Smooth everyday performance
  • Large battery with fast charging support
Cons
  • Ultra-wide camera performance is average
  • Processor not designed for heavy flagship gaming

Overall, the Redmi Note 15 review shows that the phone stands out for its display quality, camera resolution, and dependable battery life. It is a good option for users looking for a capable mid-range smartphone without moving to a more expensive flagship device.
