iPad Air M4 vs iPad Pro M4: Design, Display, Performance, Specs and Price Compared

  • Display Technology: OLED vs Liquid Retina

The iPad Air M4 and the iPad Pro M4 are two powerful tablets from Apple. Both devices use the new Apple M4 processor and support modern accessories like Apple Pencil and Magic Keyboard. However, they are designed for different users. The Air focuses on balanced performance and affordability, while the Pro focuses on premium features and advanced display technology.

Design and Build Quality Comparison

Both tablets use a slim aluminum body and Apple's modern flat-edge design. The iPad Pro M4 is thinner and slightly lighter, which makes it feel more premium in the hand.

Feature iPad Air M4 iPad Pro M4
Body Material Aluminum body Premium aluminum chassis
Thickness 6.1 mm 5.3 mm (11-inch) / 5.1 mm (13-inch)
Security Touch ID Face ID

The Pro model also uses Face ID for unlocking the device, while the Air continues to use the Touch ID sensor built into the power button. This small difference gives the Pro a slightly more advanced feel.

Display Technology Comparison

One of the biggest differences between the two tablets is the screen technology. The OLED display on the iPad Pro delivers deeper blacks and better contrast compared to the LCD panel used on the Air.

Feature iPad Air M4 iPad Pro M4
Display Type Liquid Retina LCD Ultra Retina XDR OLED
Refresh Rate 60Hz 120Hz ProMotion
Brightness Up to 600 nits Up to 1000 nits HDR

The Pro model also supports a 120Hz refresh rate, which makes scrolling and animations feel smoother. The Air display is still bright and sharp, but it focuses more on everyday use rather than professional work.

Performance and M4 Chip

Both tablets run on the powerful Apple M4 chip, which brings faster processing and better graphics performance compared to previous iPads.

Feature iPad Air M4 iPad Pro M4
Processor Apple M4 Apple M4
Graphics 9-core GPU Up to 10-core GPU
Storage Up to 1TB Up to 2TB

In everyday tasks like browsing, video streaming, and multitasking, both tablets feel extremely fast. The Pro version simply offers more power for advanced tasks such as video editing or professional creative work.

Battery Life and Accessories Comparison

Battery life is an important factor when choosing a tablet. Both the iPad Air M4 and iPad Pro M4 are designed to deliver long usage times, and Apple claims up to 10 hours of browsing or video playback. However, the overall experience can vary depending on how users take advantage of the Apple Pencil Pro and other accessories.

Feature iPad Air M4 iPad Pro M4
Battery Life Up to 10 hours usage Up to 10 hours usage
Apple Pencil Supports Apple Pencil Pro Supports Apple Pencil Pro
Keyboard Magic Keyboard support Advanced Magic Keyboard

Both tablets work well with Apple's accessories, but the Pro model is slightly better optimized for creative workflows and professional typing setups.

Connectivity and Features Comparison

Modern tablets require strong connectivity for cloud work, file transfers, and external displays. Both devices include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and optional cellular connectivity, but the USB-C port on the Pro model supports faster data speeds.

Feature iPad Air M4 iPad Pro M4
Wi-Fi Wi-Fi 6E Wi-Fi 6E
Cellular 5G support 5G support
USB-C Port Standard USB-C Thunderbolt / USB4

The Pro version supports Thunderbolt connectivity, which allows users to connect high-speed storage devices and external monitors more easily.

Price Comparison and Final Verdict

Price is one of the biggest differences between these two tablets. The iPad Air M4 starts at a much lower price, which makes it attractive for students and casual users who want strong performance without paying flagship tablet prices.

Category iPad Air M4 iPad Pro M4
Starting Price Around ₹59,900 Around ₹99,900
Best For Students and everyday users Professionals and creators
Overall Value Strong value for money Premium tablet experience

Overall, the iPad Air M4 is the better choice for most people because it delivers excellent performance at a lower cost. The iPad Pro M4 is the right option for users who want the best display, advanced connectivity, and professional-level features.

Why Should You Buy Each iPad?

Choosing between the iPad Air M4 and the iPad Pro M4 depends on how you plan to use your tablet. Both devices offer strong performance and modern Apple features, but they target slightly different types of users. Understanding the key advantages of each model can help you decide which Apple iPad fits your needs better.

1. Why You Should Buy the iPad Air M4

The iPad Air M4 is a great choice for students, casual users, and professionals who want strong performance without spending too much. It delivers fast multitasking, good battery life, and support for Apple Pencil and Magic Keyboard. This makes it ideal for note-taking, media consumption, browsing, and productivity tasks.

Because it includes the powerful M4 chip while maintaining a lower starting price, the Air offers excellent value for users who want a capable tablet for everyday work, entertainment, and creative tasks.

2. Why You Should Buy the iPad Pro M4

The iPad Pro M4 is designed for users who want the best features available in an Apple tablet. It includes an advanced OLED display, smoother 120Hz refresh rate, and professional-level performance for demanding tasks.

For creators, designers, and video editors, the Pro model offers better graphics performance and faster connectivity. These features make it more suitable for professional workflows and advanced creative applications.
