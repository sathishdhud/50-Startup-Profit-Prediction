Final Verdict: Lava Bold 2 5G vs POCO M6 Pro 5G

Buy Lava Bold 2 5G if: Your priority is a modern viewing experience and clean software. The 120Hz AMOLED display is a standout feature in this budget, offering vibrant colors and smooth scrolling that the POCO's IPS LCD cannot match. Combined with the latest Android 15, faster 33W charging, and UFS 3.1 storage, it offers a more premium feel and future-proof performance for a slightly higher price.

Buy POCO M6 Pro 5G if: You are looking for the best value and proven performance. The Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 is a capable chip for gaming and daily tasks, and the phone offers more variant flexibility. While the display and charging speed are older technology, the significantly lower price point and the inclusion of a 3.5mm headphone jack make it a practical choice for budget-conscious buyers who don't mind the MIUI ads.

Overall: The Lava Bold 2 5G pushes the budget segment forward with its AMOLED screen and clean software, justifying its slightly higher price for users who prioritize display quality and a bloatware-free experience. The POCO M6 Pro 5G remains a solid workhorse with a better price-to-performance ratio but feels dated in display technology and charging speed. If you can stretch your budget by ~₹3,000, the Lava Bold 2 5G offers a significantly more modern smartphone experience.
