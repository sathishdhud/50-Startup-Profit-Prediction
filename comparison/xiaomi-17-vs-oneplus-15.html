Final Verdict

Buy Xiaomi 17 if: You want a compact flagship smartphone with an ultra-bright display and a powerful camera system. The device features a premium AMOLED display with up to 3500 nits brightness, Leica-tuned triple 50MP cameras, and fast 100W charging with 50W wireless charging support, making it a great choice for photography enthusiasts and users who prefer a smaller flagship device.

Buy OnePlus 15 if: You want a larger display, longer battery life, and stronger gaming performance. The phone offers a big 6.78-inch AMOLED display with up to 165Hz refresh rate, a massive 7300 mAh battery, and ultra-fast 120W charging, making it ideal for gamers, heavy users, and those who prefer a bigger screen experience.

Overall: Both smartphones deliver flagship-level performance with the powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset. The Xiaomi 17 stands out with its Leica camera system, brighter display, and premium compact design, while the OnePlus 15 focuses on a bigger display, massive battery capacity, and smoother high refresh rate for gaming. If you prioritize camera quality and a compact flagship feel, the Xiaomi 17 is the better option. If you prefer longer battery life, a larger display, and stronger gaming performance, the OnePlus 15 is the better choice.
