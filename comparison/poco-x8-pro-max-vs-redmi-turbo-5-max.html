Final Verdict

Buy POCO X8 Pro Max if: You want the smoothest display and premium flagship experience. The device is expected to feature a 144Hz OLED display, powerful Dimensity 9500s chipset, and fast 100W charging, making it an excellent choice for gaming, multitasking, and high-performance usage.

Buy Redmi Turbo 5 Max if: You want the best battery life and overall value. The phone packs a massive 9000 mAh battery along with the same flagship Dimensity 9500s processor, delivering exceptional endurance and strong performance at a more affordable expected price.

Overall: Both smartphones are expected to deliver flagship-level performance with the same powerful MediaTek Dimensity 9500s chipset. The POCO X8 Pro Max focuses on a smoother display and premium features, while the Redmi Turbo 5 Max stands out with its huge 9000 mAh battery and better value for money. If you prioritize display smoothness and premium design, go with the POCO X8 Pro Max. If battery life and price-to-performance matter more, the Redmi Turbo 5 Max is the better pick.
