Apple Announces AirPods Max 2 with H2 Chip: Stronger ANC and Lossless Audio

By Dhuda Sathish • Reviewed by Maaney Editorial Team • Updated March 18, 2026
Highlights
  • Apple officially announces AirPods Max 2
  • Powered by the new H2 chip for advanced audio processing
  • 1.5× stronger Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)
  • Supports 24-bit/48kHz lossless audio via USB-C
  • New colors: Midnight, Starlight, Orange, Purple, Blue

  • AirPods Max 2 Announced
  • H2 Chip Integration

Apple has officially lifted the curtain on the next generation of its premium over-ear headphones. The AirPods Max 2 have been announced, featuring a significant internal upgrade that brings them in line with the latest AirPods Pro. The headline change is the inclusion of the H2 chip, which promises to revolutionize the audio experience with smarter noise cancellation and improved sound quality.

Representative image of high-end over-ear headphones.

Building on the success of the original model, the new AirPods Max 2 aim to address user feedback while pushing the boundaries of acoustic performance. With the tagline "Immersive sound. Intelligent features," Apple is doubling down on the high-fidelity audio experience. The design remains largely familiar, maintaining the premium mesh canopy and stainless steel frame, but users can now choose from a refreshed palette of colors including Midnight, Starlight, Orange, Purple, and Blue.

The most significant upgrade lies under the hood with the H2 chip. This proprietary silicon enables a massive leap in Active Noise Cancellation (ANC). Apple claims the new model offers up to 1.5× stronger Active Noise Cancellation compared to its predecessor, effectively silencing louder environments like city traffic or busy offices. Additionally, the H2 chip introduces Adaptive Audio and Conversation Awareness—features previously exclusive to the AirPods Pro 2—which dynamically mix transparency mode and ANC based on your surroundings and speech.

Audiophiles have a major reason to celebrate: the AirPods Max 2 support 24-bit / 48kHz lossless audio via USB-C. This means users can connect directly to a source device via the updated USB-C port to listen to music exactly as the artist intended, without compression. This move signals Apple's continued commitment to high-quality audio standards, bridging the gap between consumer headphones and professional-grade studio monitors.

New Intelligent Features

Beyond audio quality, the AirPods Max 2 are packed with new intelligence-driven features. Voice Isolation ensures crystal clear calls by filtering out background noise, making it easier to be heard in windy or chaotic situations. A new Camera Remote function via the Digital Crown allows users to control camera functions on their iPhone or iPad, perfect for taking selfies or group shots from a distance.

Another standout feature is Live Translation, powered by Apple Intelligence. This allows the headphones to translate spoken language in real-time through the earcups, breaking down language barriers during travel or international meetings. Additionally, Studio-quality audio recording makes these headphones a viable tool for content creators who need high-quality voice capture on the go.

Pricing & Availability

The AirPods Max 2 carry a price tag of $549, maintaining the premium positioning of the line. Pre-orders are set to begin on March 25, with availability expected shortly after. While the price is high, the inclusion of the H2 chip, lossless audio capabilities, and the new suite of Apple Intelligence features make it a compelling upgrade for those deeply integrated into the Apple ecosystem.
