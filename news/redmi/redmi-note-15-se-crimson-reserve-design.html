Redmi Note 15 SE Unveiled: A ‘Crimson Reserve’ Special Edition Dripping in Gold and Vegan Leather

By Dhuda Sathish • Published: March 28, 2026 • Updated: March 28, 2026 • 5 min read
The Redmi Note 15 SE 'Crimson Reserve' edition features a premium vegan leather finish

Redmi is making a bold statement ahead of its April 2nd launch by fully revealing the Redmi Note 15 SE 5G, a device that redefines "Special Edition" through sheer aesthetics. The standout feature is undoubtedly the "Crimson Reserve" colorway, a deep, vibrant red finish crafted from vegan leather that exudes a premium and classy vibe. This striking hue is elegantly contrasted by a gold-colored frame that wraps around the phone and highlights the squircle-shaped camera island, mirroring the design language of the standard Note 15 while carving out a distinct, luxurious identity. With a slim 7.35mm profile and a curved display, the device promises a sophisticated in-hand feel that punches above its expected weight class.

Underneath the flashy exterior, the Note 15 SE appears to pack a serious performance punch, largely mirroring the hardware of its more expensive sibling. The device is expected to inherit the Note 15’s core arsenal, including a 6.77-inch AMOLED screen with a 120Hz refresh rate and a peak brightness of 3,200 nits, ensuring a fluid visual experience. Powering the unit is likely Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset, paired with a robust 5,520mAh battery that supports 45W fast charging, alongside a versatile 108MP dual-rear camera setup and IP65/IP66 durability ratings—essentially offering a flagship-level experience at a fraction of the cost.

The squircle camera module design is consistent with the Redmi Note 15 series

However, the "SE" badge comes with the traditional trade-offs designed to hit a lower price point, likely under Rs 20,000. Following the precedent set by the Note 14 SE, this Special Edition may arrive with a slightly trimmed memory configuration—rumored to be 6GB of RAM compared to the standard model's 8GB—and notably, might ship without an in-box charger. While the base Note 15 starts at Rs 22,999, this strategic maneuvering allows Redmi to target a more budget-conscious audience, offering the Note series’ renowned performance to those willing to compromise slightly on RAM and accessories for a significantly lower entry price.

Beyond the raw specifications, the market positioning of the Note 15 SE is a calculated move in the highly competitive sub-20K segment. By introducing a vegan leather finish—a material usually reserved for flagship devices—Redmi is attempting to democratize premium design. This move puts pressure on competitors like Realme and POCO, who often focus solely on raw performance metrics in this price bracket. The 'Crimson Reserve' edition isn't just a color option; it acts as a differentiator that signals quality and attention to detail, potentially swaying buyers who prioritize style and in-hand feel over exhaustive spec sheets. It suggests that Redmi views the budget segment not just as a utility space, but as a category ripe for aesthetic innovation.

On the software front, the device is expected to ship with the latest HyperOS, optimized to run smoothly even on the 6GB RAM variant. This optimization is crucial, as memory management will be key to the user experience on the SE model. For the average consumer, the reduction in RAM might be a negligible trade-off when weighed against the benefits of the 120Hz AMOLED display and the efficiency of the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3. The display alone is a massive selling point, offering deep blacks and vibrant colors that make media consumption a joy—a feature often cut to save costs in budget phones. Ultimately, the Note 15 SE represents a shift towards "experience-first" budget phones, where the quality of the screen and build takes precedence over raw multitasking horsepower.

Key Specifications

Display 6.77-inch AMOLED, 120Hz refresh rate
Processor Snapdragon 6 Gen 3
Camera 108MP dual rear camera
Battery 5,520mAh with 45W fast charging
Expected Price Under ₹20,000
