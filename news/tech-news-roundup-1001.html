Motorola Users Report Issues After Android 16 Update

By Dhuda Sathish • Updated March 16, 2026
Highlights
  • Edge 50 series affected by Android 16 update
  • Devices becoming bricked or facing motherboard failure
  • Users advised not to upgrade until fix is released

Many Motorola smartphone users are reporting serious problems after installing the Android 16 update. The issue mainly affects devices in the Motorola Edge 50 series, including the Motorola Edge 50 Fusion.

According to several user reports, phones that were updated from Android 15 to Android 16 are facing multiple problems. Some users claim their devices became completely bricked, while others say the motherboard stopped working after the update.

In addition, many users are experiencing fast battery drain, and some say their phones are not charging properly after installing the update.

Since these issues appear to be related to the software update, users who are currently running Android 15 are advised not to upgrade to Android 16 for now until Motorola releases a stable fix.

If the problem becomes widespread, Motorola may need to provide free repairs through service centers for affected devices.

OnePlus Nord 6 and Nord CE6 Expected to Launch Soon

Highlights
  • OnePlus Nord 6 may feature Snapdragon 8s Gen 4
  • Massive 9000mAh battery rumoured
  • Launch expected in April 2026

OnePlus is reportedly preparing to launch the next generation of Nord series smartphones. The upcoming devices are expected to be the OnePlus Nord 6 and OnePlus Nord CE6.

Leaks suggest that this time OnePlus may use Qualcomm Snapdragon processors in both devices. Expected chipset details: OnePlus Nord 6 – Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 and OnePlus Nord CE6 – Snapdragon 7s Gen 4.

Another major highlight could be the large battery capacity. Reports suggest the Nord 6 may feature a battery close to 9000mAh, which would be one of the biggest batteries in a OnePlus smartphone.

The camera system may remain similar to the Nord 5, and a telephoto lens may not be included.

OnePlus has also teased a launch package that appears to include a smartphone, smart tag, and wireless earbuds. The devices are expected to launch in India around April 2026.

Xiaomi May Launch Xiaomi 17T and 17T Pro Soon

Highlights
  • Xiaomi 17T series to be more affordable
  • Price range expected ₹40,000 – ₹60,000
  • MediaediaTek flagship chipsets likely

Xiaomi recently launched the Xiaomi 17 series in India, including the Xiaomi 17 and Xiaomi 17 Ultra. However, the high pricing of these devices has raised concerns among buyers.

The Xiaomi 17 is currently priced around ₹87,000–₹88,000, which places it in the premium flagship segment.

To offer more affordable flagship phones, Xiaomi may bring back its T-series lineup. Previously, Xiaomi launched phones like Xiaomi 11T and Xiaomi 11T Pro.

Now reports suggest that the company could launch Xiaomi 17T and Xiaomi 17T Pro in the near future. Expected highlights include a Price range of ₹40,000 – ₹60,000 and Processor from MediaTek flagship chipsets.

A Leica partnership is expected for the camera. If launched, these phones could offer flagship features at a more affordable price compared to the standard Xiaomi 17 models.

Huawei Tablet Appears on Flipkart, But Service Concerns Remain

Highlights
  • Huawei tablet listing spotted on Flipkart
  • Sold via third-party importers
  • No official service centers in India

Huawei smartphones are currently not officially sold in India, but some of the company’s products such as smartwatches and TWS earbuds are still available in the market.

Recently, a Huawei tablet listing appeared on Flipkart, indicating that the brand’s products are still being sold in India through third-party importers.

These devices are reportedly imported by a company called Rajendra Trading Company, which brings Huawei products from international markets and sells them in India.

Although the products are genuine Huawei devices, there is an important concern: Huawei does not currently operate official service centers in India.

Because of this, buyers may face service and repair issues if something goes wrong with the device. Consumers who plan to purchase Huawei tablets should consider this lack of after-sales service before buying.
