Galaxy S27 Ultra Camera Rumors: Why That Viral 3X Periscope Claim Is Almost Certainly Wrong, and What Samsung Is Actually Building

By Dhuda Sathish | Published: April 1, 2026 | 6 min read
The Galaxy S27 Ultra is at the center of conflicting camera rumors — here is what is credible and what is not.

The Galaxy S27 Ultra is still months away from launch, but social media is already flooded with explosive camera claims — the most viral being that Samsung is packing a 1/1.28-inch 3X periscope sensor with a 10cm minimum focusing distance. It sounds like a dream spec sheet for mobile photography enthusiasts. The problem? No credible leaker has backed any part of this claim, and the available evidence strongly suggests it originated from a speculative specs database rather than an actual supply chain source. Meanwhile, the real and significantly more believable camera story — a potential 1/1.12-inch 200MP main sensor with LOFIC technology — is getting buried under the noise. Here is a clear breakdown of what to trust and what to ignore.

The 1/1.28-inch 3X periscope claim has zero backing from established Samsung insiders and appears to be speculative.

Let us address the periscope rumor directly. A 1/1.28-inch sensor inside a 3X periscope module would be an industry-first — no smartphone manufacturer has achieved this at any price point, let alone in a mass-produced flagship. The claim surfaced through third-party specs aggregator sites that frequently auto-generate specifications for unannounced devices based on wishful thinking rather than factory intelligence. More importantly, every established Samsung insider tracking the S27 Ultra's development points to the telephoto setup remaining unchanged from the S26 Ultra, which itself carried forward the same 3X (10MP) and 5X (50MP) periscope configuration from the S25 Ultra. The S26 Ultra actually moved in the opposite direction by increasing the minimum focusing distance on its periscope to prioritize long-range quality over close-up capability, making a sudden reversal to a 10cm macro-focused periscope highly improbable.

The camera upgrade that actually has substance comes from a completely different direction. Chinese leaker Digital Chat Station (數碼閒聊站), who has a reliable track record with Samsung sensor information, reported that Samsung LSI is developing a new 200-megapixel sensor with a 1/1.12-inch format — notably larger than the 1/1.3-inch ISOCELL HP2 used in the S26 Ultra. This was independently corroborated by multiple Hong Kong tech publications including *Mobile Magazine*, *ePrice*, and *AM730*. The standout feature is LOFIC (Lateral Overflow Integration Capacitor) technology, which integrates a microscopic overflow capacitor into each pixel to store excess electron charge before highlight clipping occurs, delivering dramatically improved native dynamic range without relying on multi-frame HDR processing. LOFIC is also rumored for the Xiaomi 17 Ultra, suggesting Samsung is positioning it as a premium flagship component available to multiple OEMs.

The 1/1.12-inch 200MP LOFIC sensor is the credible camera upgrade to watch for the Galaxy S27 Ultra.

However, even the main sensor rumors require careful interpretation. Some reports reference a sensor called the ISOCELL HP6 at 1/1.3 inches, leading many to conflate it with the larger 1/1.12-inch LOFIC chip. These are almost certainly two distinct sensors at different development stages — the HP6 likely being an iterative refinement of the current HP2/HP3 lineage (possibly destined for the S26 Ultra or a foldable), while the LOFIC sensor represents an entirely new architecture. The S27 Ultra would be the logical candidate for the more advanced chip, but until Samsung officially details its silicon roadmap, the exact pairing remains educated speculation rather than confirmed fact.

Beyond individual sensors, early social media commentary from tech observers suggests Samsung may use the S27 Ultra to pivot back toward hardware-first marketing after the S26 Ultra's heavily AI-centric pitch. A near-1-inch main sensor with LOFIC would fit that narrative perfectly — it is a tangible, measurable improvement that shows up in direct camera comparisons, unlike generative AI features that are subjective and harder to demonstrate side-by-side. Combined with the expected retention of the S Pen, ultrawide refinements, and the usual chipset progression, the S27 Ultra appears positioned as a refinement year focused on camera hardware substance rather than software spectacle.

The viral 1/1.28-inch 3X periscope with 10cm focusing is unsupported speculation that should be dismissed. The credible story is a potential 1/1.12-inch 200MP LOFIC main sensor — a real hardware upgrade that, if realized, would meaningfully improve dynamic range and low-light performance. Samsung's telephoto setup is almost certainly staying the same.
