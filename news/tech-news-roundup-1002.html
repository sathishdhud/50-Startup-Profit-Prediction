Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra May Introduce Advanced LOFIC Camera Technology

By Dhuda Sathish • Updated March 17, 2026
Highlights
  • Samsung may add LOFIC camera technology to Galaxy S27 Ultra
  • Technology improves dynamic range and exposure control
  • Feature previously seen in Xiaomi 17 Ultra camera system

Samsung is reportedly preparing a major camera upgrade for its future flagship smartphone, the Samsung Galaxy S27 Ultra. According to new reports, the company may introduce LOFIC (Lateral Overflow Integration Capacitor) camera technology to improve image quality.

LOFIC technology helps balance bright and dark areas in photos, which significantly improves dynamic range. This means images captured in challenging lighting conditions may look more natural without excessive brightness or shadow loss.

The same camera technology was recently seen in the Xiaomi 17 Ultra, where it demonstrated improved exposure handling and better color accuracy. If Samsung adopts this technology, it could enhance the overall camera performance of its upcoming flagship phones.

Although Samsung has not officially confirmed the feature, industry reports suggest the company is exploring advanced camera sensors for its next-generation Galaxy smartphones. More details about the Galaxy S27 Ultra camera system are expected closer to the device launch.

Huawei Mate 80 Series May Introduce Smart Call Button Feature

Highlights
  • Huawei Mate 80 may introduce smart call controls
  • Call buttons move automatically based on hand position
  • Designed to improve one-handed smartphone usage

Huawei is reportedly working on a new feature for its upcoming Huawei Mate 80 series. According to early reports, the company may introduce a smart call button interface that adapts depending on how the user holds the phone.

When an incoming call appears, the answer and reject buttons may automatically move to the side where the user’s thumb is located. This could make it much easier to answer calls quickly with one hand.

The system is expected to detect hand placement using the phone’s antenna sensors. By identifying which side of the phone is being held, the interface can shift the controls to the most accessible position.

This feature could be especially helpful for people who prefer one-handed smartphone operation or users with smaller hands. Huawei has not officially confirmed the feature yet, but it may appear in the upcoming Mate 80 lineup.

Apple Launches AirPods Max 2 With H2 Chip and Live Translation

Highlights
  • AirPods Max 2 launched with new H2 chip
  • Improved audio quality and performance
  • New live translation feature introduced

Apple has officially launched the next generation of its premium headphones, the AirPods Max 2. The new model brings several upgrades including an improved chip and new smart features.

Highlights
  • Apple acquires Motion VFX
  • Better titles and transitions for Final Cut Pro
  • More editing plugins expected in the future

Samsung Pauses Tri-Fold Phone Sales in South Korea After Display Issues

Highlights
  • Samsung tri-fold phone sales paused in South Korea
  • Users reportedly facing display-related issues
  • Company may relaunch after design improvements

Samsung has reportedly paused sales of its experimental tri-fold smartphone in South Korea. According to local reports, some users experienced problems related to the device’s folding display.

The tri-fold phone is one of Samsung’s most ambitious foldable designs, featuring a display that folds in multiple sections. However, early users reportedly noticed screen durability and reliability issues during regular usage.

Because of these concerns, Samsung is said to have temporarily halted production and sales while engineers analyze the problems. The company is expected to improve the display technology before bringing the device back to market.

Samsung has not officially confirmed the timeline for the next release, but industry analysts believe the company will continue developing advanced foldable and multi-fold smartphone designs in the future.
